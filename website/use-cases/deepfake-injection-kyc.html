Live Deepfake Image Injection to Evade Mobile KYC Verification

Facial biometric authentication services are commonly used by mobile applications for user onboarding, authentication, and identity verification for KYC requirements. The iProov Red Team demonstrated a face-swapped imagery injection attack that can successfully evade live facial recognition authentication models along with both passive and active liveness verification (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liveness_test) on mobile devices. By executing this kind of attack, adversaries could gain access to privileged systems of a victim or create fake personas to create fake accounts on banking or cryptocurrency apps.

Why this record has no ERS

This AIDEFEND SecureFlow record converts MITRE ATLAS case study AML.CS0033 into a defense-mapped attack flow. It documents the observed attack path and the applicable AIDEFEND controls, but it does not establish one bounded assessed deployment with a configuration, a complete evidence set, and measured control performance. Assigning IVP, ORP, ACI, or ERS values would invent system-specific evidence. The attack steps, ATLAS technique references, and the exact upstream defense set are retained as candidate evidence routes only.
